FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started.

How long does account setup take?▼
Account verification typically takes under 5 minutes for individuals and 1-2 business days for businesses. Our automated KYC/AML checks keep things fast while staying compliant.
What currencies do you support?▼
We support 40+ currencies including USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, and major emerging market currencies. New currencies are added regularly based on customer demand.
Is my money safe?▼
Absolutely. Funds are held in segregated accounts at tier-1 banks. We're PCI DSS Level 1 certified, SOC 2 Type II audited, and fully licensed in every jurisdiction we operate in.
Can I integrate with my existing systems?▼
Yes. Our RESTful APIs and SDKs for Python, Node.js, Ruby, Go, and Java make integration straightforward. Most teams are up and running within a day.
